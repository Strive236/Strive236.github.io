abstract: >-
  FASA bridges low-level forensic traces and high-level semantic understanding for image manipulation localization. It combines adaptive dual-band DCT frequency cues with CLIP-based manipulation-aware semantic priors, then integrates them through a semantic-frequency adapter and a prototype-guided, frequency-gated decoder. Experiments show state-of-the-art localization, strong cross-generator and cross-dataset generalization, and robustness to common image degradations.
